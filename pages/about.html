About GyaanSetu

Bridging the knowledge gap — one micro-tutorial at a time.

Our Mission

GyaanSetu (Hindi: ज्ञानसेतु — "Bridge of Knowledge") is a free, lightweight web platform where college students share micro-tutorials with rural learners across India. We believe that knowledge should not be locked behind paywalls or limited by internet speed.

Millions of rural students in Maharashtra and across India lack access to quality study materials and coaching. While urban college students have knowledge and skills to share, there was no simple, free, lightweight platform designed specifically for low-bandwidth users — until now.

GyaanSetu is designed to work on 2G internet connections, targeting rural or low-income learners who cannot afford coaching classes. All tutorials are 100% free — forever.

The Problem We're Solving

📵

No Internet Access

Most villages rely on 2G or have no connectivity at all. Our platform loads on the slowest connections.

💸

Unaffordable Coaching

Private coaching costs ₹500–2000/month — beyond the reach of most rural families.

📚

Skills Gap

Coding, digital literacy & English are scarce or absent in most government schools.

🌐

Digital Divide

Urban students get quality resources. Rural students get nothing. GyaanSetu bridges this gap.
